Privacy Policy & QA Testing Notice

Last updated: November 2026

Important QA Notice: Chorbajar is a frontend-only practice environment. No real user personal data, payment card numbers, or passwords are transmitted to external servers. All authentication and transactional data reside strictly within your local browser storage.

1. Information Storage

All state information including your user profile, cart items, wishlist, saved shipping addresses, and order history are persisted locally via your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage APIs.

2. Automated Testing Data Isolation

Because this application is tailored for software testing and Playwright test automation, you can use the "Reset Demo Data" feature at any point to restore the application state to factory defaults.

3. Simulated Payments

Payment options (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Cash on Delivery, Net Banking) are purely simulated workflows designed to verify form validations, order generation, and stock decrement logic.
